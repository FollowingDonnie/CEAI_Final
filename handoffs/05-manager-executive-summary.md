# Purple Manager Executive and Operational Summary

**Stage:** 5 - Manage  
**Product:** Northstar Rack Upgrade Advisor  
**Decision:** **LAUNCH a controlled partner-discovery and pilot-readiness phase. Do not launch a production compatibility service.**  
**Decision date:** 11 August 2026

## 1. Executive decision

Launch the verified fictional demonstrator for invited conversations with a small number of specialist gym-equipment retailers or manufacturers in the UK and Ireland. The immediate objective is to test buyer urgency, data ownership, catalogue readiness, and willingness to run a bounded same-brand pilot.

This is not approval for real compatibility guidance, an open commercial launch, partner branding, broad catalogue ingestion, cross-brand matching, safety advice, or automated outreach. Commercial impact remains unproven. A partner pilot may proceed only after the human founder approves the target, data boundary, wording, owners, safeguards, measures, and cost.

## 2. Opportunity and product summary

The Researcher found a credible but unquantified problem: rack attachments can be version- and condition-sensitive, while customers must reconcile matrices, product pages, and support information. Direct manufacturer evidence shows that fit errors can contribute to returns, but no reliable UK/Ireland incidence, cost, buyer urgency, or willingness-to-pay figure was established.

The organisation therefore pivoted from a broad home-gym planner and cross-brand equipment consultant to a narrower opportunity: a retailer-owned, consumer-used, same-brand rack upgrade advisor for existing rack owners. The approved product requires exact rack identity, uses governed relationship records, preserves unknowns, and produces a printable Upgrade Decision Sheet rather than only conversational text.

The deployed Northstar proof of concept uses fictional equipment and a controlled external registry. A deterministic engine owns the compatibility outcome. A bounded OpenAI Decision Guide may explain validated current-sheet facts but cannot create or alter status, conditions, readiness, commerce, sources, or purchase gating.

## 4. Handoff integrity and major decisions

The four specialist handoffs preserve a coherent decision chain:

1. **Research gate - PASS:** The same-brand upgrade opportunity follows from independently checked evidence and explicit comparison with broad planning, first-purchase recommendation, and cross-brand alternatives.
2. **Design gate - PASS after revision:** The structured decision flow, bounded AI role, live-data dependency, trust behaviour, and testable acceptance criteria trace directly to the accepted research.
3. **Build gate - PASS:** The deployed critical path works; live external values affect outputs; tests pass; secrets remain server-side; failures are handled; and the product matches the accepted design.
4. **Communication gate - PASS:** Claims are bounded by research and verified capability, the buyer and pilot ask are consistent with the opportunity, and no production readiness or real-company affiliation is implied.

Major scope decisions were retained across every handoff: exact same-brand identity rather than dimensional inference; deterministic compatibility authority; four evidence states including useful unknowns; no hidden current-data fallback; compatibility freshness separate from commerce freshness; fictional data for the public proof; and human review before any real partner use.

## 6. Commercial value and proposed pilot model

The buyer proposition is that a retailer's approved compatibility knowledge could become easier for customers to act on without giving AI authority to invent fit. Potential value may appear through more appropriate attachment handoffs, fewer avoidable compatibility contacts, clearer escalation, and fewer compatibility-related returns. None is yet a supported outcome claim.

The proposed commercial path is:

1. Conduct a small set of human-led discovery interviews across ecommerce/commercial, support, catalogue/PIM, technical product, and compliance ownership.
2. Select one retailer or manufacturer that confirms a material workflow and can name an authoritative data owner.
3. Audit one same-brand rack family and bounded attachment set before configuring a private prototype.
4. Compare the guided flow with the partner's existing matrix, product pages, or support route in observed user tasks.
5. Run a controlled live pilot only after data, product, support, legal/safety, security/privacy, accessibility, and brand owners approve.
6. Expand one family at a time only when measured value and sustainable governance are demonstrated.

No fixed price or ROI case should be proposed before discovery establishes data effort, integration scope, operating ownership, buyer value, and measurable baselines.

## 7. Operational requirements

- A named compatibility-record owner with approval authority, review cadence, change history, contradiction handling, and an incident pause process.
- Stable exact product/version identifiers, legacy identification clues, authorised attachment relationships, conditions, negative outcomes, provenance, review dates, and explicit unknowns.
- Separate authorised commerce integration for price, stock, and product links; commerce must never determine compatibility.
- Private partner environments and least-privilege credentials; the public demonstration Sheet is not the assumed production architecture.
- Monitoring for registry availability, source-contract failures, stale/review-due records, Guide verification failures, customer-facing errors, and CORS/configuration regressions.
- Existing support, installation instructions, and escalation routes retained throughout any pilot.
- Human review by product/technical, product-safety/legal, privacy/security, accessibility, support, brand, and commercial owners before customer-facing use.
- Defined rollback, record correction, customer communication, and pilot-stop procedures.

## 8. Risk register

| Risk | Owner | Mitigation and decision rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer pain or commercial magnitude is too small | Founder / commercial lead | Run discovery before integration work. Stop if sellers cannot evidence a material workflow or baseline. |
| Existing matrices already solve the job | Product lead | Compare against the current route in observed tasks. Continue only on observable added value. |
| Exact identities or relationships are incomplete | Partner product-data owner | Audit one family; missing relationships remain unknown. No positive claim without approved provenance. |
| Records become stale, contradictory, shifted, or mistyped | Compatibility owner / engineering | Plain-text contract fields, source-contract tests, review dates, monitoring, sampling, and immediate pause on integrity failures. |
| Customers overread compatibility as safety approval | Product/technical and legal owners | Persistent scope language, direct instructions, explicit limitations, support escalation, and specialist sign-off. |
| AI explains beyond the validated facts | Engineering / product owner | Minimum validated context, read-only tool, fixed schema, allowlisted references/actions, customer-language guard, fail closed, and browser regression review. |
| External registry, AI, Render, or Pages is unavailable | Engineering / support | Registry failure suppresses current claims; AI failure preserves the deterministic sheet; monitor availability and keep retry/support paths. |
| Cross-origin or deployment configuration breaks the journey | Engineering | Automated preflight test, public-browser smoke test, documented allowed origins, and deployment checklist. |
| Public data or branding implies an unauthorised relationship | Founder / legal / brand | Use fictional data publicly; require written partner authorisation and private configuration before real records or branding. |
| A broad rollout magnifies weak governance | Manager / partner sponsor | One-family scope, staged gates, measured expansion, rollback, and authority to stop. |

## 9. Measures of success

### Discovery and readiness

- Percentage of interviewed sellers confirming a material compatibility workflow.
- Ability to name authoritative technical and data owners.
- Availability of a compatibility-contact/return taxonomy and usable baseline.
- Percentage of one-family relationships with approved provenance and current review status.
- Rates of missing, stale, contradictory, malformed, and unresolved records.

### Customer task

- Exact-rack identification completion and error rate.
- Decision-sheet completion and time to outcome versus the existing route.
- Comprehension of confirmed, conditional, incompatible, and unknown states.
- Condition completion, unresolved/escalation rate, and support-summary or print use.

### Commercial and operational

- Eligible attachment handoff and conversion against an agreed baseline.
- Compatibility-related contacts, handling time, cancellations, and returns using agreed definitions.
- Evidence-owner effort, review-due compliance, record incidents, and resolution time.
- Successful registry retrieval, fail-closed events, Guide availability/verification failures, and customer complaints.

Report every measure with its population, period, sample, baseline, and attribution limits. Expansion requires measured customer or operational value, sustainable governance, acceptable failure behaviour, and formal owner approval.

## Final judgement

Northstar is a credible and unusually honest proof of concept: it demonstrates that AI can reduce interpretation effort while governed records and deterministic rules retain authority. The technical product is ready for demonstration. The commercial opportunity is ready for discovery, not for claims of proven demand or impact. Launch the learning process; keep production authority with the humans who own the products, evidence, and risk.
