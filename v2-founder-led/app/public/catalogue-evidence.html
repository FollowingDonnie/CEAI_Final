Northstar governed prototype specification

Northstar and its products are fictional. The catalogue demonstrates a research-led planning method and does not imply affiliation with any equipment manufacturer.

How the catalogue was created

Product patterns and realistic ranges were triangulated from current manufacturer product pages and manuals reviewed in the project research. Original Northstar names, SKUs, prices, descriptions and simplified geometry were then created as controlled prototype records. They are not copied commercial products or manufacturing drawings.

What the values mean

Dimensions, operating envelopes, rack interfaces and compatibility relationships are governed prototype inputs. Fictional EUR prices and stock are timestamped commercial inputs. Missing values remain explicitly unavailable. A compatibility approval requires a controlled relationship; dimensional similarity alone is never presented as approval.

Limits

The planner checks the recorded rectangular room geometry and encoded clearances. It does not assess a building, mounting surface, installation, exercise technique, floor loading or product safety. Current product instructions and appropriate professional advice remain necessary.

Research record

The project research register cites primary manufacturer, OpenAI, Google, Konva, Three.js, W3C, Irish and EU sources used to define the method. Access dates and decisions are retained in the project evidence dossier.
