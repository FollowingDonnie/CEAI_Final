# Research Inputs

Run `deep-research-prompt.md` in Deep Research and place the complete result in
this folder as either:

- `deep-research-report.docx`, or
- `deep-research-report.md`.

The Researcher treats the dossier as a strong input, not unquestionable truth.
It must verify decisive claims, identify evidence gaps, and clearly separate
sourced facts, inference, recommendations, and unknowns.
