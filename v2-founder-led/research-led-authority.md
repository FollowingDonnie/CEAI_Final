# Research-Led Design and Implementation Authority

This document records the founder's final instruction before execution and
clarifies how `founder-charter.md` must be interpreted.

## Fixed by the Founder

The founder fixes the problem and intended value:

- make planning or upgrading a home gym substantially easier through natural
  conversation;
- use governed live equipment data rather than invented product facts;
- reason about room constraints, equipment combinations, attachment compatibility,
  and budget;
- provide a useful visual representation of the proposed training space;
- expose uncertainty and avoid unsupported safety or compatibility claims;
- demonstrate a credible product concept for customers and equipment retailers.

These outcomes and trust boundaries may not be discarded.

## Determined by Research

Research is the primary authority for the best way to realise the vision. It may
revise assumptions in the founder charter about:

- the exact discovery conversation and required inputs;
- catalogue breadth, category mix, field schema, and evidence model;
- recommendation, compatibility, geometry, clearance, layout, and quote logic;
- which interactions belong in chat versus visible controls;
- the balance and behaviour of 2D and 3D planning;
- technical architecture, libraries, APIs, storage, caching, and deployment;
- MVP sequencing and which advanced interactions should follow later;
- evaluation, accessibility, failure handling, and customer-facing explanations.

A change must be supported by accepted research, recorded in the decision log,
and preserve the founder-fixed outcomes. The charter's earlier implementation
suggestions are starting hypotheses, not instructions to ignore stronger evidence.

## Designer Authority

The Designer should implement the strongest coherent product experience supported
by the accepted research brief, even when it differs from the founder's or
pipeline author's initial mental picture. The Designer must explain the evidence,
trade-off, and outcome preserved. It should not present several unresolved visions
when the evidence supports a clear recommendation.

## Maker Authority

The Maker should use the best evidence-supported architecture and implementation
strategy. If implementation evidence exposes a better approach than the design
specification, the Maker may propose and implement it after the Manager verifies
that it preserves the research conclusion and founder outcome. The change and its
validation must be recorded; technical convenience alone is not sufficient.

## Manager Test

For any apparent conflict, ask:

1. Is this an outcome/trust boundary or merely an assumed implementation?
2. What does the accepted research support?
3. Does the proposed change improve delivery of the founder's product value?
4. Is the reasoning traceable and testable?

Research-led improvement passes. Silent scope reduction, unsupported novelty, or
a pivot to a different product fails.
