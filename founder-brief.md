# Founder Brief

## Starting challenge

People building a home gym can struggle to compare equipment because dimensions, clearances, technical specifications, and attachment compatibility are inconsistent or incomplete. This can make it difficult to choose equipment for a particular room, budget, training goal, or future upgrade path.

Investigate whether this problem supports a useful AI-enabled product that could be demonstrated to gym-equipment retailers, manufacturers, or related businesses.

## Founder observations

- The founder experienced this problem while building a home gym.
- A commercially interesting system might act as a digital equipment consultant rather than a basic chatbot.
- Mirafit was suggested as one possible research lead because its public product information appeared comparatively detailed.
- Limiting retrieval to a controlled source may be more reliable than attempting to search the whole internet.
- A guided conversation paired with structured controls and a visible, editable output may communicate more value than a chat transcript alone.

These are hypotheses, not decisions. The Researcher must test them independently and may reject them. The Designer must choose the interaction model from the accepted research rather than treating any founder suggestion as a fixed specification.

## Required outcome

The five-agent organisation must identify, design, build, communicate, and evaluate one coherent product opportunity. The result must include a working public prototype that demonstrates live external data being queried at runtime and produces a tangible customer output rather than only conversational text.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- Use exactly five AI-agent roles: Researcher, Designer, Maker, Communicator, and Manager.
- Preserve a clear, sequential handoff between agents.
- Do not assume Mirafit, the home-gym architect concept, or any proposed data source is the correct answer before research.
- Do not crawl the unrestricted internet as part of the customer-facing runtime.
- Do not fabricate product specifications, prices, compatibility, market evidence, or citations.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed facts, reasoned inferences, and unknowns.
- Keep credentials and API keys outside source control.
- Require human approval before deployment, publication, spending money, or contacting an external organisation.

## Commercial lens

The prototype should make the underlying capability easy to demonstrate to a gym-equipment company. The Researcher must determine whether the strongest customer is a consumer, retailer, manufacturer, or another segment, and the later agents must follow the evidence.
